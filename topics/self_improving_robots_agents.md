# Self-Improving Robots & Agents

Systems that get better from their own experience: agentic skill/reward/code evolution (agents that diagnose failures, write skills or rewards, and keep what works), real-world RL, autonomous data collection, curiosity, and recursive improvement of agents. Newest first within each section; a few entries also appear in other topics.

Sections: [Agentic robot self-improvement](#agentic-robot-self-improvement) · [Real-world RL and autonomous policy improvement](#real-world-rl-and-autonomous-policy-improvement) · [LLM-designed rewards, environments and sim-to-real](#llm-designed-rewards-environments-and-sim-to-real) · [Self-improving AI research and digital agents](#self-improving-ai-research-and-digital-agents) · [Curiosity, exploration and data engines](#curiosity-exploration-and-data-engines)
